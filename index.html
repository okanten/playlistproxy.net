Service Discontinued

Spotify has restricted their API in a way that sadly leaves PlaylistProxy with no option to continue. The service is no longer functional and the backend/API has been shut down.

Thank you for using my service, I hope I protected the privacy for people wanted to stay anonymous online.

Appreciation

Due to limited server resources and spotifys 10k playlist per account limit (which we hit twice) I knew I couldn't handle tons of users, so I never advertised or announced it anywhere when I launched this. Seeing that the service got naturally discovered is something that makes me happy. It definitely made my week when I discovered that Grindr used this service back in 2024.

So long, and thanks for all the fish
